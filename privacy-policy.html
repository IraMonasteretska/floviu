Privacy Policy

At Floviu, we believe your focus journey is personal. Everything we build is designed to help you better understand your attention—not to distract you or profit from your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Floviu, we may collect the following information.

Account Information
  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication provider (Apple, Google, Firebase)
  • Subscription status
Focus Session Data
  • Session duration, start & end time
  • Focus timer settings
  • Selected Focus Tags & Soundscape
  • Session ratings & reflections
  • Focus Quality Score
  • Focus World progression
  • Focus history
Focus Protection Data
  • Protected apps opened
  • Number of distraction attempts
  • Whether a reflection prompt was shown
  • Whether you continued or returned to focus
Calendar Data
  • Calendar events (if you connect)
  • Event titles
  • Event start and end times
Device Information
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous crash reports
  • Performance diagnostics

Note on Focus Protection: Floviu never collects the content you view inside other applications.

Note on Calendar: Floviu never edits or deletes calendar events without your permission. Calendar data is only used to display your schedule inside Floviu, help plan focus sessions, and generate personalized focus insights.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Floviu experience
  • Save your focus sessions and history
  • Grow your Focus World
  • Generate personalized AI-powered Focus Insights
  • Discover focus patterns
  • Improve app performance
  • Process subscriptions
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Improve the security and reliability of Floviu

We only process the information necessary to provide these services.

4. AI-Powered Focus Insights

Floviu uses OpenAI (ChatGPT) to generate personalized Focus Insights. To provide these insights, AI may process information related to your focus sessions, including:

  • Focus session duration
  • Focus Tags
  • Session ratings
  • Focus times
  • Soundscapes
  • Distraction patterns
  • Focus history

This information is used solely to generate personalized recommendations, such as your Focus Formula, Best Focus Window, Focus Quality Score, helpful focus patterns, and personalized recommendations to improve your focus habits.

AI-powered insights are designed to support your focus journey — not make decisions on
your behalf.

5.We Never Sell Your Data

Your attention deserves better.

Floviu does not sell your personal information, sell your focus history, build
advertising profiles from your focus sessions, or share your personal focus data with advertisers.

Your information is only used to provide and improve Floviu.

6. Third-Party Services

To provide Floviu and its features, we work with trusted third-party service providers.


Authentication
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Sign in with Google
Analytics & Crash Reporting
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
Cloud Infrastructure
  • Supabase
AI Services
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)
Subscription Payments
  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play

Each provider processes information according to its own Privacy Policy.

7. Subscription Payments

Subscriptions are securely processed through Apple App Store and Google Play. Floviu never stores or has access to your payment card information. Please refer to Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies for payment-related information.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Regular security monitoring

While no service can guarantee absolute security, protecting your information is one of our highest priorities.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide Floviu and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Focus Data
  • Stored for as long as your Floviu account remains active
Deleted Accounts
  • Personal information and focus history deleted without undue delay, except where legally required
Analytics & Crash Reports
  • Anonymous data retained for up to one year to improve Floviu's performance, stability, and reliability

10. International Data Transfers

Some of the third-party services used by Floviu may process or store personal data outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Floviu stores your focus sessions and primary application data on servers located within the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

Certain service providers (such as authentication, payment processing, analytics, crash reporting, and AI services) may process limited personal data outside the EEA. Where such transfers occur, we rely on appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where applicable), or other lawful transfer mechanisms under the GDPR. Your personal data is protected using industry-standard encryption and security measures designed to safeguard its confidentiality and integrity.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Object to certain processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@floviu.com.

12. Your Choices

You can choose to:
  • Delete focus sessions
  • Disconnect calendar integrations
  • Disable Focus Protection
  • Change notification preferences
  • Cancel your subscription at any time
  • Delete your Floviu account

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data in accordance with this
Privacy Policy.

13. Children's Privacy

Floviu is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use our services independently. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify users through the app or by other appropriate means before those changes take effect.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always indicate the latest version.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we process your personal information, we're happy to help.
