Why Focus World exists

Your effort shouldn't disappear
when the timer ends

Traditional focus timers reset when the session ends. Your effort shouldn't.

You focused for 30 minutes. The timer ends. Tomorrow, it feels like you're starting from zero again.

That's why we created Focus World. A place where every focused session leaves something behind.

Small focused moments slowly become something you can actually see.

Because progress isn't always obvious in real life.

Sometimes you need to see how far you've come.

How we treat your data

Privacy matters to us

Focus data can reveal a lot about how you spend your time.
We believe it should be treated with care.

Stored in the EU

Your focus sessions and primary application data are securely stored on servers located within the European Union.

Built with privacy in mind

We use industry-standard security measures and build Floviu with privacy and GDPR requirements in mind.

Your attention isn't the product

We don't sell your data, build advertising profiles, or share your focus history with third parties.

2023

TheBusinessDive
begins

We started TheBusinessDive with one goal: helping people find productivity tools that genuinely improve the way they work. Over the following years, we tested around 100 productivity apps every year, exploring everything from focus and note-taking to project management, calendars, and time tracking.

2025

The idea for Floviu

After years of testing productivity apps, we started imagining a calmer, more personal way to help people focus. We believed people didn't need another timer—they needed a better relationship with their attention.

Jan-Apr 2026

Listening before
building

Before designing Floviu, we spent months reading thousands of conversations about focus, distractions, and productivity. The struggles people shared became the foundation for every feature we built.

Jun 2026

The first Floviu
prototype

The first version of Floviu came to life, bringing together a Focus Timer, Soundscapes, Focus Protection, Insights, and the first version of Focus World.

Aug 2026

Floviu launches

A calmer way to focus. Built from real experiences. Ready to help people spend less time fighting their attention and more time understanding it.
