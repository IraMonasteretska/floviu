About Floviu

We built Floviu
because focusing shouldn't feel like
a fight with yourself

Your thoughts won't slow down. You open an app without
thinking. Minutes disappear before you even realize it.

We believe focus should feel calmer, not harder.

Built around how focus
actually feels

We didn't start by building features. We started by listening to how people experience focus.

“I literally can't concentrate.”

“I open apps without realizing it.”

“I don't know where the last 30 minutes went.”

“I don't know why I can focus some days and not others.”

Those experiences shaped Floviu.

Our principles

A calmer way
to focus

Floviu follows a few simple principles.

  • No punishment
  • No guilt for breaking a streak
  • No complicated system to learn

Create the right environment.

Understand your attention. Grow into your focused self.

Why Focus World exists

Your effort shouldn't disappear
when the timer ends

Traditional focus timers reset when the session ends. Your effort shouldn't.

You focused for 30 minutes. The timer ends. Tomorrow, it feels like you're starting from zero again.

That's why we created Focus World. A place where every focused session leaves something behind.

Small focused moments slowly become something you can actually see.

Because progress isn't always obvious in real life.

Sometimes you need to see how far you've come.
