Refund Policy

Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store

If you purchased Floviu through the Apple App Store, all billing and refunds are handled by Apple. To request a refund, please visit Apple's refund portal or contact Apple Support.

If you're unsure how to proceed or have any questions, feel free to reach out to us. We'll be happy to point you in the right direction and help however we can.

Subscriptions purchased through Google Play

If you purchased Floviu through Google Play, billing and refunds are handled by Google. Refund eligibility is subject to Google's refund policies. Please visit Google Play Support for assistance.

If you need any guidance or have questions about your subscription, don't hesitate to contact us. Even though refunds are handled by Google, we're always happy to help.

Website purchases (future)

If Floviu subscriptions become available directly through our website, this Refund Policy will be updated to reflect our refund terms.

Need help?

If you have any questions about your subscription, billing, or refunds, we're here to help. Just send us a message at support@floviu.com, and we'll do our best to assist you as quickly as possible.
